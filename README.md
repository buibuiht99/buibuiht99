# Hi, I'm Tra Bui

**Developmental Psychology PhD Student at UNLV | Human–AI Interaction | Mixed-Methods Research**

I study how people understand and interact with technology, with a focus on voice assistants and generative AI. My work combines experiments, surveys, qualitative coding, and data analysis to understand user behavior and inform design.

I'm interested in **Summer 2027 user experience research internships**, especially opportunities to study AI experiences, learning, and differences in user needs across age groups.

## Research and projects

### Voice assistant interaction
Designed and conducted a 2 × 2 Wizard-of-Oz study with **95 children and adults**, examining voice gender and response style. Combined ratings, drawings, and interviews to investigate how participants perceived the assistant and developed recommendations for age-appropriate interaction design.

**In progress:** An R workflow comparing human and automated coding of interview responses about voice assistants.

### AI and student writing
Conducting a PRISMA-guided review of generative AI in higher education writing, examining writing performance and cognitive independence.

**In planning:** A mixed-methods study using surveys, mouse tracking, and chat logs to examine when and how students use AI during writing.

### AI and human behavior
Co-leading survey research on how personality, loneliness, attachment, and executive function relate to AI use. Analyze quantitative and qualitative data and supervise two research assistants from study setup through analysis.

### Program evaluation at BeHERE NV
As a Data Analyst Graduate Assistant, I clean and recode **88 Qualtrics variables in R**, evaluate survey reliability and factor structure, and code qualitative field notes to examine behavioral health career camp outcomes. I also support Power BI reporting for behavioral health workforce planning.
